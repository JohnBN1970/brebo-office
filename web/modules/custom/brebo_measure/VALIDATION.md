# Validation evidence v0.1

For a measured feature, validation compares observations rather than replacing them.

Example:

- LiDAR observation: 1843 mm, uncertainty 2 mm;
- laser reference: 1841 mm;
- calculated difference: +2 mm.

Across repeated captures Office can later calculate bias, spread and outliers per device, operator, feature and site condition. These statistics determine whether a feature is suitable for mobile-only measurement or needs a physical reference/precision tool.
